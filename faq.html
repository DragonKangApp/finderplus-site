FINDER+ / FAQ

Good questions. Clear answers.

Answers about Finder+ availability, permissions, compatibility, trial and licenses.

Can I buy or download Finder+ now?

No. Finder+ is Coming Soon. There are no public downloads, payments, pre-orders or license delivery on this website.

When does the trial start?

Only after you choose Start Free Trial in License & Trial. It lasts 14 × 24 hours. Expiration keeps the basic free mode and all safety controls available.

How many Macs does a license cover?

One user on up to three personal Macs. This is a license term; the current App does not collect hardware fingerprints or count devices online. 1.x updates are included.

Why do I need to authorize folders?

Content operations need explicit permission through the system folder picker. Finder menu coverage does not grant content access. Finder can provide selected names and paths to the extension; authorize only the folders you need.

Does Finder+ require Full Disk Access?

No. The current implementation does not require Full Disk Access or use it to bypass folder authorization.

Are exFAT, NAS or network transfers supported?

No. Copy and Move on exFAT, SMB, NFS and WebDAV are unsupported in 1.0.0. Internal HFS+ runtime is not validated.

Are all USB devices and Intel Macs validated?

No. APFS USB validation covers one Kingston DataTraveler 3.0 on one Mac, with limitations. Intel has been compiled, but its runtime is not validated.

Does Copy preserve all file metadata?

No. Full metadata cloning is not guaranteed. ACLs, extended attributes, Finder tags and resource forks are not guaranteed. Copied trees do not preserve hardlink relationships.

Does Replace merge folders?

No. Replace replaces the entire target folder after confirmation. Keep Both creates a separate named copy; Skip keeps the original target and, for Move, the source.

What should I do after an interrupted Move?

Inspect source and target from Transfers → Needs Attention, then use the available recovery controls. Do not blindly repeat the entire Move or manually delete retained recovery files.

What happens when I remove authorization or a license?

Removing authorization does not delete your files. Removing a local license keeps folder authorizations, history and recovery records; it resumes the original trial’s remaining time or expired state. Neither grants a new trial.

Does Finder+ upload my files?

The frozen App operates locally, without a Finder+ file upload service, analytics, account or cloud sync. Offline license validation stays on your Mac. Other apps and system file providers can have their own network behavior.

Why is the Finder menu missing?

Run the main App once, use Settings → Open Extension Settings and enable Finder+. Labels differ across macOS versions. Menu coverage, Finder context and folder authorization are separate conditions; try reopening the Finder folder.

What are the refund and support terms?

Sales and support submissions have not opened. The payment provider, refund terms and verified support contact will be published before sales. No response-time commitment is currently offered.
